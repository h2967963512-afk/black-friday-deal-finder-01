How prices are checked

Every deal on this board was found and checked by hand on Friday, November 27, 2026 — Black Friday itself. Each price was read on the named store's own product page, or in a deals source that names the store and the price (9to5Mac, Mashable, CNN Underscored, PopSci, The Verge, Slickdeals, DealNews). The check time and the source are printed on every deal.

What the verdict means. New low is the lowest price the sources have ever tracked for that item. Strong deal is a deep cut, close to the lowest tracked price. Solid deal is a real saving, not near a record. Small cut is a real but modest discount — worth it only if you wanted the item anyway. A discount is only counted against the store's own usual price, never an inflated one.

Prices move all day on Black Friday. Check the price at the store before you pay — if it has moved, the deal has moved. FridayFinds takes no payment and earns nothing from these links; the links go straight to the store's product page.
